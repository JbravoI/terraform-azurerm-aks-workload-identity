# Authors and recognition

## Project creator and maintainer

This project was created and is maintained by [@JbravoI](https://github.com/JbravoI).

## Contributors

Every accepted contribution is recognised through its pull request, commit
history, and release notes where applicable. Contributors may also be listed
here with their GitHub handle and contribution area after their first
substantial merged contribution, unless they prefer not to be named.

To contribute, read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). For a security report,
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than opening a public issue.
