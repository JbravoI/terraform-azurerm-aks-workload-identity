# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a respectful, welcoming space for everyone. We
commit to participating without harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
or intimidation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive, considerate, and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent and make room for different experience levels.
- Focus discussion on the code, documentation, and ideas rather than people.
- Respect privacy; never share another person's personal, employer, customer,
  or security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, hateful or discriminatory language, threats, sexualised language
or imagery, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and bad-faith security disclosure
are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Do not disclose security vulnerabilities in a
public issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

The maintainer may remove or edit comments, reject contributions, temporarily
or permanently restrict participation, or take another proportionate action.
Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
